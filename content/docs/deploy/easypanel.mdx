title: Easypanel

---

[Easypanel](https://easypanel.io) is a self-hosted Docker deployment platform. Memos has a one-click deployment template there, as an alternative to the manual Docker deployments described in this section.

[![Deploy on Easypanel][easypanel-btn]][easypanel-deploy]

[easypanel-btn]: https://easypanel.io/img/deploy-on-easypanel-40.svg
[easypanel-deploy]: https://easypanel.io/templates/memos

The template persists data in a mounted volume and exposes Memos on your project's domain.
