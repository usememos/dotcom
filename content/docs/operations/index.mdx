Establish and test a restore procedure before your first upgrade. Memos applies database migrations at startup; after a migration, recovery depends on restoring a compatible backup rather than simply restarting the older binary. Include every attachment store still in use in that recovery plan.

For a specific failure, start with [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting). For planned database or storage changes, return to [Configuration](/docs/configuration).
